Privacy Policy - Tikvo

Last updated: November 2026

Tikvo at https://tikvo.store respects your privacy.

Data we collect

  • Firebase UID, email, display name
  • TikTok OpenID, display name, avatar
  • Encrypted TikTok tokens in Cloudflare D1
  • Your niches, scripts, stories (titles, dialogues, characters), render jobs
  • Photos and videos you upload for your videos, and copies of generated voice-overs, stored in Cloudflare R2

Retention

  • Uploaded photos, videos and voice-over copies are kept for at most 7 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Rendered videos are stored as release assets on GitHub; the oldest are removed once more than 300 are kept.
  • Scripts and stories stay in your account until you delete them.

Third-party processing

Scripts and story texts are sent to text-generation models (Cloudflare Workers AI and free model providers) to produce suggestions, stories and voice-overs. Text is not used to train models by Tikvo.
